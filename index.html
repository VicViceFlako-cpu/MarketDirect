About Us

Built on customer relationships.

An independently operated sales and marketing company focused on professional representation and sustainable growth.

Our company

Market Direct was built on the belief that meaningful customer relationships drive lasting business growth. Through strategic field marketing, professional sales representation, and ongoing team development, we connect businesses with consumers while creating opportunities for individuals to grow professionally.

We are based in the Bronx and work across New York City and Northern New Jersey.

Mission

Connect service providers with the households they serve, through people who represent them well.

Our operational approach

  • Training before the field. New representatives complete training before they sell on their own.
  • Daily structure. Teams meet every day to review goals, territory and standards.
  • Managed territories. Coverage is planned so campaigns are organized and trackable.
  • Mentorship. Experienced team leaders coach newer representatives.
  • Follow-through. We track each customer order through to installation.

Victor Declet

Founder & President

Victor Declet is an entrepreneur and business leader with over 14 years of experience in direct sales, marketing, customer acquisition, and business development.

As Founder and President of Market Direct LLC, Victor has built his career around developing high-performing sales teams, creating business opportunities, and connecting established brands with the consumers they serve.

Throughout his career, Victor has gained extensive experience across telecommunications, residential internet, wireless services, and consumer-focused marketing campaigns. His leadership approach emphasizes accountability, professional development, operational excellence, and consistent execution.

Victor believes that the foundation of a successful organization lies in its people. His commitment to mentorship and leadership development centers on empowering individuals to strengthen their skills, take ownership of their performance, and grow into positions of greater responsibility.

Under his leadership, Market Direct continues to focus on expanding its market presence, strengthening business relationships, and building a culture defined by integrity, ambition, and measurable results.

Our Services

Professional customer acquisition.

We plan, staff and manage face-to-face sales campaigns for established service brands.

  • Direct Sales & Customer Acquisition

    Connecting brands with qualified consumers through professional, face-to-face sales campaigns. Our representatives present the service, answer questions and complete the sign-up with the customer.

  • Field Marketing & Brand Representation

    Representing products and services in targeted communities while maintaining professional customer interactions. The brand's reputation is in our hands at the door, and we treat it that way.

  • Telecommunications Marketing

    Supporting customer acquisition for residential internet, connectivity, and telecommunications services.

  • Sales Training & Development

    Recruiting, onboarding, training, and developing representatives to operate effectively in competitive markets.

  • Territory & Campaign Management

    Organizing field teams, coordinating geographic coverage, and managing daily sales activities.

Professional standards

How our representatives are expected to work

  • Ethical representation. Accurate information about the service and its pricing. No pressure.
  • Screening and training. Representatives are background screened and trained before working a territory.
  • Accountability. Customers can contact the company directly about any interaction.
